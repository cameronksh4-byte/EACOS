# Office printer shows offline

## Steps for the user
1. Make sure the printer is on and its display shows no error such as a paper jam or low toner.
2. On your computer, open **Settings > Printers & scanners**, select the printer, and choose **Open print queue**. Cancel any stuck jobs.
3. Remove the printer, then add it again from **Add device** and pick the printer named on its label.
4. Try printing a test page.

## Escalate to Tier 2 when
- The printer is offline for everyone on the floor.
- The printer display shows a hardware error code.
