# Handoff packet for every escalation

Every escalation, every time, the person taking over gets this packet. Collect what's missing with short questions; write "unknown" rather than guess.

1. User name and contact
2. Device (type, and asset tag if known)
3. Location: office, home, or job site
4. Scope: one user, or who else is affected
5. Exact error text, in English, as shown on screen
6. When it started, and what changed
7. What the user already tried
8. Business impact and urgency
9. For security or patient-data cases: what was clicked, opened, entered, approved, or sent, and when

**Never include:** a password, an MFA code, or patient data (PHI).

Tell the user plainly that the case is being escalated, who will follow up, and that they don't need to keep trying fixes. The named human contact is configured per client.
