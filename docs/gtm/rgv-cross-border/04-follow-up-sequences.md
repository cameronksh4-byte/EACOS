# 4. Outbound Multi-Channel Follow-Up Sequences

**When to use this sequence:** you visited in person, had a real conversation, and they said some version of *"Send me more info, talk next week."*

**The point:** each touch earns the next by being **useful, short and specific to what they told you.** The in-person visit is still the main event; WhatsApp and email exist to get you back through the door.

## Ground rules

1. **Ask permission in person:** *"¿Le puedo mandar la información por WhatsApp?"* ("Can I send you the info on WhatsApp?") No permission, no WhatsApp; use email only.
2. **Send from a real person.** Use the rep's WhatsApp Business app, 1:1. No bulk blasts, no automation tools. (On the WhatsApp Business Platform API, business-initiated messages need approved templates and opt-in, which is another reason to send these by hand.)
3. **Match their language.** If they spoke Spanish with you, write in Spanish. If they mixed, you can mix.
4. **Use their words.** Every touch references the pain they described, logged in the CRM as `pain_story`.
5. **Stop the moment they reply,** and respond like a human, not like a sequence.
6. **Respect their time.** Send Monday–Friday, 9:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m., never during the morning bridge rush, **never on Sundays**.
7. **Email basics (CAN-SPAM):** a real physical address in the signature, a plain way to opt out, and honor it right away.
8. **Every example must be real.** "Catch of the week" stories come from actual pilots, **sanitized and used with permission**. Before you have one, use a clearly labeled sample.

## The sequence at a glance

| Touch | When | WhatsApp | Email | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1: Thank you + the info** | Same day, within 2 hours of the visit | Short thanks + one-pager PDF + their pain in one line | One-pager + compliance memo, recap in their words | Deliver exactly what they asked for, fast |
| **2: Value, no ask** | 3 business days later | One real (sanitized) catch, with a screenshot | The same story, a bit more detail | Show what a catch looks like on *their* type of freight |
| **3: The "next week" they asked for** | 7–8 days after the visit (the "next week" they named) | A specific visit time + the pilot offer + an easy out | The same, with two time options | Get back in the door to set up the pilot |

## What happens after Touch 3

| Their response | What you do |
|---|---|
| **"Yes, come by."** | Confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before. Bring a pre-provisioned pilot kit. Run the [10-minute setup](03-shadow-pilot-blueprint.md#32-the-10-minute-zero-it-setup). |
| **"After the season."** | Thank them. Log the exact date they gave and **check back on that date**, no earlier. |
| **"No."** | "Thank you for telling me straight. If anything changes, you have my number." Mark them closed-lost with the reason. Add them to the monthly nurture only if they gave email permission. |
| **No reply at all** | **Don't send a 4th message.** Drop by in person once within 30 days (in a prime window), with a new real catch story printed out. If there's still nothing after that, move them to the monthly "Catch of the Month" email. |

## Variant: cold-storage 3PL or yard

Use the same three touches and swap the pain points:

- Replace "before the entry goes to ACE" with **"before the truck pulls into your yard."**
- Replace "corrections and holds" with **"detention minutes, blocked dock doors and reefer fuel."**
- Touch 2 story angle: *"The yard got the alert 3 hours early and kept the dock door free for a load that was ready."*
- Add a referral line to Touch 3: *"Which brokers do you work with most? We'd love an introduction if the pilot helps."*
