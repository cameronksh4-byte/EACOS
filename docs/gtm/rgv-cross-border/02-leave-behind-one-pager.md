## Why the security section says "commitments," not "guarantees"

The brief asked for "compliance guarantees." On paper, **commit to what *we* control** (where data lives, who can see it, what we never do, how long we keep it) and put it in the contract. **Never guarantee the broker's compliance**, because only the broker can deliver that. A licensed broker will notice the difference immediately, and the honest version builds more trust than the bold one.
