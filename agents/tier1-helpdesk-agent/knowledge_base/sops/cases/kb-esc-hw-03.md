# PC won't turn on / hardware failure signs

Area: Monitors / Hardware. Handling: Escalate-only. Give only the safe first steps below, collect the handoff packet (KB-POLICY-HANDOFF), and hand off to a person. Don't troubleshoot further.

**What users say:** “It won't turn on” · “It makes a noise and shuts off” · clicking · burning smell

**Ask first:** One user, or everyone? When did it start, and what changed?

**Safe first steps:**
1. Check the power cable, outlet, and (laptop) charger light. Hold the power button for a full shut-down, then try once more.
2. Ask about noises, smells, heat, and whether anything was dropped or spilled.

**If that doesn't fix it:** Do not open the machine. Do not keep retrying.

**Escalate to a person when:** Escalate immediately — possible hardware failure. A human diagnoses, arranges a loan machine, and backs up before any further work if a drive may be failing. Burning smell, smoke, or heat: unplug, move away, do not use it.

Shared baseline knowledge base: generic steps. A client's own SOP for the same issue wins. Never collect a password or MFA code; never make admin or permission changes.
