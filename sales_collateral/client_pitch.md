# AuditGate
### Catch billing errors before you pay, and keep your documents on your own computer.

---

**You've probably heard that AI can read invoices and paperwork for you. You've also probably decided not to upload your customers' bank details to a chatbot.** We think you made the right call. AuditGate is built for business owners who think the same way.

## What it does

Give AuditGate an invoice, a contractor's bid or a work order (PDF, Excel or plain text). In a few seconds it:

1. **Pulls out the important details:** vendor, dates, every line item and the totals.
2. **Checks the math:** quantity × price, line items against the subtotal, subtotal plus tax against the total, and due dates against invoice dates.
3. **Gives you a clear verdict:** ✅ *Pass*, 🟡 *Needs review* or ⛔ *Fail*, with a plain-English reason for each flag.

> *"Line 1: 10 × $45.00 = $450.00, but the invoice says $540.00."*
> AuditGate finds the $90 mistake. It won't quietly smooth it over.

## Why you can trust it with private documents

| Your concern | How AuditGate handles it |
|---|---|
| "I don't want my data on someone else's servers." | By default **everything runs on your own computer**, with no internet connection needed. |
| "What if I do want a more powerful AI model?" | You can turn that on. Before anything is sent, names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, bank and card numbers and tax IDs are **swapped for placeholders**. The real values stay with you and are put back into the results afterwards. |
| "Can I see exactly what gets sent?" | Yes. The dashboard shows the exact text the AI saw, next to a count of what was hidden. |
| "Can someone hide instructions in a document to trick the AI?" | Every release is tested against documents that try exactly that. |

## Why you can trust the answers

AI tools sometimes make things up. AuditGate is designed so that's very hard to miss:

- **It only reports what's printed on the page.** If a total is missing, AuditGate marks the document as incomplete. It does not guess a number.
- **The math checks are ordinary arithmetic, not AI.** They give the same answer every time.
- **It's tested before every update.** A library of tricky real-world documents (wrong totals, missing fields, foreign currencies, documents packed with personal data, hidden instructions) runs on every release. An update can't ship if accuracy drops or if a single piece of personal data would have leaked.

## Who it's for

Property managers, contractors, clinics, restaurants, accounting and bookkeeping firms: anyone handling invoices and bids who needs a second pair of eyes but can't risk sending client data to an outside service.

## What it looks like day to day

- Drag a file into a simple dashboard in your web browser.
- Export the results to JSON, with Excel coming soon.
- *Optional:* connect it to your existing tools through a local connection that stays on your own network.
