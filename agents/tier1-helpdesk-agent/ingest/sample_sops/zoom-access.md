# Get Zoom on your computer

## When to use
You need Zoom for meetings. Zoom doesn't need manager approval.

## Steps for the user
1. Wait for the confirmation that your Zoom access has been set up.
2. Open Self Service (Mac) or Company Portal (Windows) and install **Zoom Workplace**.
3. Open Zoom and choose **Sign in with SSO**, then enter your work email.

## Escalate to Tier 2 when
- Zoom says your account isn't licensed an hour after setup.
