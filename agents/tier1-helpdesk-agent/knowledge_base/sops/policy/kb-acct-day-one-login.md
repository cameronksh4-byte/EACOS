# New starter can't sign in on day one

Area: Passwords / Accounts. Handling: Guide the user, then escalate if the account or licence is the problem.

**What users say:** "It's my first day and I can't log in" · "My new account doesn't work"

**Collect:**
1. Which step fails: the computer sign-in, email, MFA setup, or a specific app
2. The exact error text, in English, as shown
3. Whether MFA enrolment was completed

**Escalate to a person when:** the account doesn't exist, the licence is missing, or MFA enrolment can't be completed. That's an admin fix, not a user fix. Never ask for or send a temporary password in chat; a person delivers it through a separate channel.
