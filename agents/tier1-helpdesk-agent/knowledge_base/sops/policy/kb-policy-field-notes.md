# Field notes by type of business

**Clinics:** any case touching patient systems, the EHR, or patient data (PHI) is an immediate escalation (KB-ESC-TRIGGER-10). Never troubleshoot PHI access in chat, and never ask for patient details.

**Contractors and logistics:** field, mobile, shared printer or scanner, and VPN-from-a-job-site cases are common. Always ask where the user is (office, home, job site, truck) and whether they're on Wi-Fi, mobile data, or a hotspot.

**Professional services:** Outlook, Teams, and shared-drive cases dominate. "Can a colleague in the same role open it?" is the fastest scope check.
